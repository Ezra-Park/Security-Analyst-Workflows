# 02 - Cybersecurity Audit Workflow: Controls and Compliance Assessment for Botium Toys

This section demonstrates the workflow involved in conducting a cybersecurity audit using a **controls and compliance checklist**. This type of audit is crucial for rapidly assessing an organization's current security posture against foundational controls and adherence to key compliance regulations.

---

## Scenario: Controls and Compliance Assessment for Botium Toys

In this simulated scenario, an initial cybersecurity assessment was conducted for **Botium Toys** using a structured checklist. The audit's primary objective was to identify the presence and effectiveness of essential security controls and to determine adherence to critical compliance best practices (PCI DSS, GDPR, and SOC principles).

The following document, `Audit_Report_Example.md`, presents the findings from this checklist assessment. It details the current state of various technical and organizational controls, highlights areas of non-compliance, and provides strategic recommendations for improving Botium Toys' overall security posture and reducing risk.

---

## Workflow Steps Demonstrated:

1.  **Audit Planning & Checklist Selection:** Defining the scope and selecting relevant control and compliance checklists.
2.  **Evidence Gathering & Control Assessment:** Verifying the presence and initial effectiveness of security controls (e.g., through documentation review, simple checks).
3.  **Compliance Best Practice Assessment:** Evaluating adherence to specific regulatory best practices.
4.  **Finding Identification:** Documenting controls not in place or areas of non-compliance.
5.  **Recommendation Development:** Formulating strategic recommendations based on identified gaps and associated risks.
6.  **Report Generation:** Presenting the checklist results and recommendations clearly for stakeholders.
